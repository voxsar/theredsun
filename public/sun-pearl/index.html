The menu

How it is made

Three steps, no shortcuts.

Pearls cooked fresh

Black tapioca is boiled in small batches through the day and rested in brown sugar syrup, so it stays soft and chewy rather than hard in the middle.

Real fruit, not powder

Passionfruit pulp and crushed strawberry go in by hand. You can see the seeds and the fruit in the cup, because they are actually there.

Soda poured last

The sparkling lemon-lime base goes in over ice at the very end, so the drink reaches you still fizzing instead of flat.
