# Sun Pearl by The Red Sun — Uber Eats listing copy

Paste-ready copy for the Uber Eats Merchant signup and menu builder. Item names and
descriptions are deliberately keyword-loaded so the items surface in Uber Eats' in-app
menu search (search matches store name, category, item name and item description).

Prices are intentionally left blank — fill them in at upload time.

> **Note on the base:** the website and this copy describe the base as a "sparkling
> lemon-lime soda" rather than naming Sprite or 7Up. Naming another company's brand on
> your own menu is a trademark issue and Uber Eats can reject it, so keep it generic.
